Standing flags — never silently included
• [UNVERIFIED] Price / wrap competitiveness — no rate data in either corpus. Price-to-win before any gate. The Soto Cano price win does not transfer automatically.
• GAP Transportation, food service / DFAC, and executed HA-DR surge — no records in either corpus. Teaming requirements on any TO where these dominate.
• GATE No EAGLE II BOA (watch SAM.gov for on-ramp). No LOGCAP V position — "dual LOGCAP + WEXMAC reach" stays retired.
• [UNVERIFIED] NAICS 561210 registration — confirm in SAM before any BOS prime bid.
• [COLLECT] Records with no published task-order end date carry an EST end bounded by the vehicle ceiling (LOGCAP V PoP ends Mar 2, 2030; WEXMAC 2.0 base ordering ends Dec 2029, option to 2034). EST is a latest-possible date — the real PoP is a collection requirement (FPDS/GovWin) before B&P commitment.
• [SIGNAL] Disrupted records (TITUS conversions, ICE detention orders) reflect court/exec actions as of the map snapshot — perishable; re-verify.
