Methodology — the six rooms

Room 1 · WHATs
Customer decision drivers (left wall), each with an importance weight. Weights here are analyst defaults — no Section M is in hand for any candidate; replace them with the real Section M evaluation weights per pursuit before a gate review.
Room 2 · HOWs
RMGS-Versar capabilities and discriminators (ceiling), capped at nine and drawn only from the corpus. See the HOW register below for each anchor.
Room 3 · Relationship matrix
Each HOW × WHAT scored 9 (strong) / 3 (moderate) / 1 (weak) / blank (none). Every 9 cites the specific corpus record that justifies it.
Room 4 · Correlation roof
Reinforcing (+) / conflicting (−) relationships among HOWs, plus the portfolio sequencing note (below).
Room 5 · Competitive wall
RMGS vs. the named incumbent on each driver (1–5). Incumbent ratings are analyst judgment from public data, not corpus facts.
Room 6 · Basement — targets & roll-up
HOW importance = Σ(weight × score) · Fit % = Σ(weight × best score per WHAT) ÷ (9 × Σ weights) · Competitive factor = Σ(weight × RMGS 1–5) ÷ Σ(weight × incumbent 1–5) · Roll-up = Fit % × 100 × competitive factor.

HOW register (Room 2) — capabilities & corpus anchors

Every HOW is a corpus-anchored capability. H9 (partner bench) carries capability claims only — no partner performance records exist in the corpus — and is capped at a relationship score of 3.
