01 The vehicle

One IDIQ. Five ways to build partner capacity.

GLOBALCAP supports the Bureau of African Affairs (AF/RPS) and the Bureau of Political-Military Affairs (PM/GPI, PM/SA) as they strengthen partner nations' security institutions and operational capability. Task orders draw on five overarching capability areas defined in Section C of the contract — ordered separately or together, wherever the mission goes.

Forces that train themselves

Section C.2.1 — Training, mentoring & monitoring services

RMGS fields instructor cadres that make host-nation security forces self-sufficient — starting with the trainers themselves. Instructor development first; then combat lifesaver and TCCC, command-and-staff operations, small-unit leadership, and civil-military operations, with human rights and international humanitarian law woven through every program. Every course ships with a Program of Instruction, pre- and post-training evaluation, and complete progress reporting.

Where the mission is observation rather than instruction, RMGS stands up monitoring teams for cease-fire, human rights, and protection of civilians in austere post-conflict environments — fully self-supported with communications, protective equipment, transportation, life support, and medical evacuation, interoperable with UN agencies, the AU, NGOs, and peacekeeping forces.

9 course lines in scope POI + pre/post eval, every course Monitors — ceasefire · HR · PoC NET to depot-level maintenance Surge for urgent requirements

Logistics where there are no roads

Section C.2.2 — Logistics support services

GLOBALCAP logistics means moving personnel, heavy equipment, rations, ammunition — including high explosives — and casualties by ground, sea, and air, fixed- and rotary-wing, into places with dirt runways and broken roads. RMGS provides the labor, parts, fuel, drivers, and supervision to do it end-to-end.

Behind the movement sits the machinery: air-freight and warehouse operations, consolidated receiving, joint inventories, ISR equipment operation and maintenance, and full-spectrum sustainment — food, water, POL, billeting, sanitation, and field medical support. The RMGS-Versar MPJV already runs base operating support at Soto Cano Air Base under WEXMAC 2.0 — the same muscle, applied to the same kinds of austere environments.

Air · sea · ground incl. rotary/fixed-wing CRP CONUS → OCONUS delivery ITAR + customs documentation 100% inventory, items > $200 Medical support in combat ops

Equipment, certified & delivered

Section C.2.3 — Equipment procurement

Procurement under foreign assistance rules is a compliance discipline as much as a supply one. RMGS attests that every item matches the government's specification — no counterfeits — and provides blast and ballistic certification from reputable U.S. or foreign sources for body armor, helmets, vehicles, and protected facilities, with all foreign-assistance procurement-restriction documentation completed at the task-order level.

From individual kit to conventional-munitions and IED disposal equipment, gear arrives export-ready, joint-inventoried, and ready for the operational environment it was bought for.

Zero counterfeit tolerance Blast & ballistic certification Export & ITAR compliant Joint inventory at delivery
  • Vehicles — military and civilian
  • Individual equipment & personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • Unmanned aerial surveillance (UAS) equipment
  • Medical equipment
  • Conventional munitions disposal (CMD) equipment
  • Improvised explosive device disposal (IEDD) equipment
  • Logistics support equipment, radios & generators

Design. Build. Anywhere.

Section C.2.4 — Design & construction services and activities

From site assessment to ribbon cutting: licensed architects and engineers deliver complete, accessible, functional designs — civil, geotechnical, structural, electrical, mechanical, fire protection — and construction crews turn them into training camps, aircraft hangars and runways, marine piers, warehouses, medical facilities, schools, and libraries, at new sites or existing host-nation facilities, including rapid response to fast-developing situations.

Versar's engineering depth inside the MPJV pairs with RMGS program discipline: mobilization plan, quality-control plan, baseline schedule, submittal register, risk mitigation, SITREPs, safety and demobilization plans — the full deliverable set the COR expects on day one.

Licensed A&E design Permanent + temporary facilities Rapid response tasking 9 PM deliverables per project
  • C.2.4.1A&E DesignSite assessment & survey, civil & geotechnical, architectural, structural, electrical, mechanical, fire protection
  • C.2.4.2ConstructionBuild or refurbish — training camps, hangars & runways, boat storage, warehouses, medical facilities, piers, schools, libraries

Accountable to the last line item

Section C.2.5 — Property maintenance

The quietest activity carries the loudest audit consequence. RMGS maintains all U.S. Government-funded and contractor-acquired property in accordance with USG property regulations — on the books, in condition, and accounted for until formal transfer to a DoS-designated representative removes it from contractor property records.

Bi-monthly property books, end-of-task-order accountability packages, and standing coordination with project employees and subcontractors keep every task order clean at closeout.

USG property regulations Books → transfer & acceptance Every 2 months property books

  • C.2.5.2Project MeetingsConduct and attend work-coordination meetings with project employees and subcontractors
